Official AOR Groups

The Archdiocese of Roblox oversees several official groups. Joining these groups on Roblox is the best way to get involved in our community.

Primary Groups

The Catholic Church (TCC)

The primary group of the Archdiocese. This is where all main masses, ceremonies, and official communications take place. All members of the AOR must be a member of this main Roblox group.

View on Roblox

Knights of Columbus (KOFC)

A fraternal service order within the AOR. The Knights of Columbus focus on charity, unity, and fraternity, providing honor guards for ceremonies and organizing community service events.
